PDFuvo

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

PDFuvo is a PDF editor for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: your documents never leave your device, there is no account, and we only collect anonymous usage statistics.

Your documents

Every PDF you open, create or edit in PDFuvo stays on your iPhone. Editing, signing, merging, compressing and every other tool run entirely on your device. We never upload, read or store your documents or signatures on any server. Files leave your device only when you choose to share them, for example with Share.

No account

PDFuvo has no login or registration. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are useful and what to improve, PDFuvo uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics:

  • Which screens are opened and which tools are completed (for example "compress"). File names and document contents are never included.
  • Basic device information: device model, iOS version, app version and language.
  • Approximate country or region, which Google derives from a shortened (masked) IP address. We never access GPS or precise location.
  • A random identifier for this installation of the app (the Firebase app instance ID). It resets if you delete and reinstall the app.

We do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV), set a user ID, link this data to you, use it for advertising, track you across other apps or websites, or sell it. We only see aggregated reports. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Google Privacy Policy; analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you scan a document.
  • Photos — only the photos you pick to turn into a PDF, or to save to your library.
  • Face ID — only to unlock the Vault. Face ID data is handled by iOS and never available to PDFuvo.

Children

PDFuvo is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete your documents in Settings → Clear Documents (Vault files are kept until you remove them from the Vault), or delete everything by deleting the app. Because analytics data is anonymous and not linked to you, we can't identify or retrieve an individual's records.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
