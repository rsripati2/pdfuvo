Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. PDFuvo has no login. Open the app and start working.

Where are my files stored?

On your iPhone, inside PDFuvo. They are never uploaded. To send a file somewhere, open it and tap Share.

How do I open a PDF?

Tap any tool on the Home screen (for example Annotate) and pick a PDF from the Files app. After that it appears under Recent — tap it to open, and the bar at the bottom has every tool.

Why can't I select text after compressing?

Compression saves each page as an image to make the file much smaller, so the text in the compressed copy can't be selected or searched. Your original file is unchanged.

I forgot the password I added to a PDF.

PDFuvo can't recover PDF passwords. If you saved the protected copy as a new file, the original is still unprotected.
